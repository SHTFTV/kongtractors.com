Can I hire equipment with an operator?+

Yes, you can enquire about equipment only, a machine with an operator, or a contractor to complete the job. The provider, availability and terms are confirmed before booking.

Can mechanics and hydraulic repair businesses join?+

Yes. Describe what you repair, your workshop location, mobile service area and whether you offer equipment pickup and return.

Do I have to be a dealer to sell equipment?+

No. Equipment businesses and independent owners can apply. A sales and service business can choose both sales and repairs.

What area does Kong cover?+

Kong currently focuses on the Fraser Valley, Metro Vancouver and nearby British Columbia communities. Include your job location or business service area in your enquiry.
