Kubota KX040-5: pair the attachment with the hydraulic setup

A breaker, auger and tilt tool make different demands on an excavator. Build the KX040-5 enquiry around the exact attachment, fitted circuits and documented handover.

By Kong Journal · November 18, 2026

A compact excavator can arrive with the right model badge and still need a careful attachment discussion. On a KX040-5 booking, the useful questions concern the fitted hydraulic circuits, the attachment's own requirements and who confirms the combination before work begins.

This is an original Kong setup guide for an existing model. Kubota's current Canadian product page and manufacturer brochure provide the facts; the enquiry examples are Kong editorial planning advice. No local availability, firsthand test or performance improvement is established here.

Start with AUX1 and the optional second circuit

Kubota's brochure identifies AUX1 as standard and AUX2 as an optional auxiliary circuit. It also describes adjustable maximum auxiliary flow and memory for up to five attachment flow settings. Those details help frame a quote, but do not prove which options are installed on a particular hire or used machine.

Give the provider the attachment's make, model and documentation. Ask which circuit and controls it requires, whether the proposed excavator has that equipment, and what additional plumbing or installation is included. Keep the answer beside the attachment line on the quotation.

IDENTIFY THE COMPLETE COMBINATION

Excavator serial number → installed auxiliary circuits → attachment model → documented requirements → responsible supplier.

Treat a saved flow preset as part of the handover

The Canadian product page describes up to five programmable attachment flow presets and a 7-inch display with auxiliary-flow access. That is a convenience feature to discuss at handover. A preset name or a previous operator's saved setting is not evidence that it suits a newly connected attachment.

Useful question: “Can you show the competent operator which setting belongs to this exact tool and confirm it against the attachment documentation?” Ask the supplier to record that identification with the machine and tool. This article gives no pressure setting, flow adjustment procedure or instruction to change a live hydraulic system.

Bring the mounting and hydraulic questions together

Ask the supplier to review the coupler or mounting arrangement, hose connections, required controls and the attachment's specified hydraulic requirements as one package. Describe the intended work as well as the tool's name. A tilt attachment, an auger and a breaker deserve individual confirmation.

For a proposed rental, list the exact attachment and any installation or control kit separately. Clarify who is responsible for fitting it, demonstrating it and supplying the applicable manuals. If an attachment comes from a different provider, make sure both parties agree who checks the combined setup.

Take photographs of identification plates before ordering consumables or requesting service. A clear record is more useful than trying to identify a component from a distant jobsite photograph.

Plan around the actual site and machine configuration

Send the provider photographs of the working area, access restrictions and the intended material or task. Confirm the actual machine's canopy or cab, tracks, blade, coupler and attachments. Transport planning needs the complete supplied combination rather than an assumed weight for every KX040-5.

If lifting or working near a structure is part of the job, have a competent provider assess the relevant configuration and site. Use the machine's applicable lifting information and operating manual. Digging force, machine weight and a picture of another excavator do not establish a permitted lift.

Leave the handover with a service route

Agree which operator checks are required before use, how a fault should be reported and who authorizes service work. Record the machine hours, attachment condition and contact details at delivery. Keep the supplied machine and attachment documentation with the job; for this excavator, the applicable excavator operator manual governs machine operation.

Discuss any observed leak, damaged hose or abnormal behaviour with the provider before continuing work. Do not use a general planning guide as authority to diagnose a pressurized system or improvise a repair.

A CLEARER FIRST ENQUIRY

Job location, task, exact tool, machine identification, circuit requirements and handover arrangements—in one message to the provider.
