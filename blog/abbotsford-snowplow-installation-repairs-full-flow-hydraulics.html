Contact Full Flow about the equipment you actually have. The shop can confirm the suitable scope, parts and appointment options for your equipment.

Planning a snowplow installation for an Abbotsford truck?

Start with the truck year, make, model and configuration, plus the plow make and model if you already own one. Include photographs of the equipment and any existing mounting or control hardware. A plow being moved from another vehicle needs a compatibility assessment; the previous installation does not establish the fit on the next truck.

WESTERN’s vehicle-matching tool uses vehicle-specific information to identify approved applications. Its document library also separates installation instructions and mount kits by equipment. Those resources illustrate why the installation discussion needs more than “it is a one-ton truck.” Follow the instructions for the actual manufacturer and equipment combination.

Ask Full Flow what can be assessed at the shop, what information it needs beforehand, and which parts or additional work may be required. Confirm the installation scope and estimate directly before arranging the work.

Book repairs before a small fault disrupts the route

A plow that worked last season still deserves attention before it returns to service. Describe symptoms clearly: when the problem occurs, which function is affected, whether it is intermittent, and what has changed since the equipment last worked normally. Photographs or a short video can help explain the issue to the shop.

The manufacturer’s preseason checklist is a useful basis for that service conversation. WESTERN includes mounting, electrical connections, hydraulic condition and cutting-edge wear. BOSS’s inspection checklist likewise covers hydraulic components, structure, mounting and operation. Exact inspection and service requirements depend on the plow and vehicle; the shop should assess the equipment and follow the applicable manuals.

Send the details to Full Flow and ask for the right next step. The shop can confirm workshop appointments and discuss any transport or urgent service arrangements it offers.
