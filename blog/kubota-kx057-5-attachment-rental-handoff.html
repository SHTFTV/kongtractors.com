Kubota KX057-5: plan access and attachment handoff

November 28, 2026 · Kong Journal

A compact excavator hire brief covering site access, the exact rental configuration, and powered attachment checks.

Measure the access route first

Kubota Canada lists the KX057-5 at 1,960 mm overall width and 5,550 kg operating weight. It lists a maximum digging depth of 12 ft 9 in. Confirm dimensions for the tracks, blade and attachment on the unit you will receive. Record the narrowest gate, any turning constraints, and the available space in the work area. The listed width is a starting point for a quote.

Match the attachment to the actual machine

Send the attachment model, coupler requirements and hydraulic specifications from its manufacturer. Ask the supplier to confirm which circuit and configuration the unit has, whether the attachment is supported, and who will check the fitted combination with the operator before work.

Show what the machine needs to do

Describe the material, desired trench or grading work, and where excavated material will go. Point out fences, walls, traffic and nearby obstacles so the rental desk can discuss room for movement. Published maximum depth does not specify reach with a particular attachment or setup.

Put the agreement on the delivery record

Record the machine model, attachment, access assumptions and receiving contact. Ask who to call if the delivered combination differs from the quote. Have the supplier resolve the discrepancy before the job relies on that equipment.
