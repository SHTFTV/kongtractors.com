TRACK LOADERS / RENTAL HANDOFF

Kubota SVL75-3: check the tracks before taking a rental

November 27, 2026 · Kong Journal

A compact track loader handoff checklist for documenting track condition, undercarriage wear and the ground conditions before a rental starts.

Inspect the machine you will actually take

Ask the rental desk to identify the unit by serial number and record the installed track type. With the machine parked and shut down, walk around it together. Photograph both tread faces and the accessible sides of the undercarriage. Note visible cuts, missing tread, exposed reinforcement and uneven wear. Record each side separately so a return inspection can use the same views.

Explain the ground and the moves

Tell the supplier whether the work crosses finished pavement, loose stone, wet fill or tight turns around walls. Include any transitions across curbs or ramps. Ask whether the installed tracks and planned route suit the surface, and who will handle any surface protection. Do not rely on the model name to establish how a specific rental is equipped.

Agree on who handles adjustment

Ask the supplier to confirm track condition and tension against the manual for that unit. Ask who you should call if the operator sees a track shift, new damage or packed material that cannot be cleared safely. Record that contact in the machine handoff sheet before dispatch.

Use the same record at return

Keep the serial number, pickup photos, agreed condition notes and operator contact together. Take the return photos from the same angles. If there was a new symptom, document when it started and the operating surface at the time so the supplier can assess it.
